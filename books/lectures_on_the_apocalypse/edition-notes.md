# Lectures on the Apocalypse — About This Digital Edition

## The original book

*Lectures on the Apocalypse, or Book of the Revelation of St. John the Divine*
by Frederick Denison Maurice.
Second Edition, Macmillan and Co., London, 1885.

## This edition

Produced 2026-08-05 from a page scan of the printed book, as part of a
project to make F. D. Maurice's works freely available in every
useful digital form. The guiding principle throughout was fidelity
to the printed text: Maurice's own spelling, capitalization,
punctuation, ligatures, and typography rule, except where a printing
error was identified and deliberately emended (each such change is
listed below).

## How this text was made

The transcription pipeline combines automated tools, AI models, and
page-by-page human review:

- **OCR and conversion.** The scan was OCRed (ocrmypdf/Tesseract)
  and converted to structured text (marker), then cleaned by a
  language model (claude-haiku-4-5-20251001) working chunk by chunk.
- **Human review of every machine change.** Every word the cleanup
  model altered was reviewed against an image of the printed line.
  Footnotes were verified against the printed page and renumbered
  uniformly.
- **Quotation marks** were normalized to the printer's own
  conventions and validated by a nesting checker; every remaining
  irregularity was inspected against the page image. The final text
  has fully paired quotation marks.
- **Italics were restored** (the OCR text layer records none) by a
  vision model (claude-sonnet-5) cross-checked against a
  deterministic measurement of letterform slant on the page images,
  with per-word statistical calibration derived from the book
  itself. Every page was then reviewed by a human editor against
  the print. This edition carries 709 italic spans.
- **Small capitals and ligatures** (æ, œ) are reproduced as
  printed.
- **A whole-book audit** (claude-sonnet-5) checked structure,
  headings, footnotes, and Greek; its findings were reviewed by a
  human editor against the print before any were applied.

The text contains 1 footnote and 51 small-caps renderings of the print.

## Emendations

Places where this edition deliberately differs from the printed
book, each a correction of an apparent printing error, verified
against the page image:

- p. 92: ‘is’ → ‘it’
- p. 93: ‘II.’ → ‘IV.’
- p. 93: ‘the oil and the wine.’ → ‘the oil and the wine."’
- p. 122: ‘fhe’ → ‘the’
- p. 162: ‘days’ → ‘day’
- p. 218: ‘jndgments’ → ‘judgments’
- p. 245: ‘their tongues for pain.’’ → ‘their tongues for pain."’
- p. 264: ‘religions’ → ‘religious’
- p. 295: ‘contiuue’ → ‘continue’
- p. 330: ‘whosover’ → ‘whosoever’
- p. 366: ‘tn’ → ‘in’

Printer's oddities that are *not* errors were left exactly as
printed (including inconsistent proper-name spellings and the
original's own numbering gaps).
